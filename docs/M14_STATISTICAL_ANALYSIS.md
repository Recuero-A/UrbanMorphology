# Milestone 14 statistical analysis

Every number is from `outputs/m14/REPORT.md` / `report.json`, generated by `scripts/m14_report.py` from the stored runs. The analysis follows the pre‑registration (`M14_EXPERIMENTAL_PROTOCOL.md` §6 and Amendments 1–2). Nothing here was re‑specified after the confirmatory results. The sealed test splits were never read.

## 1. Units, sample sizes and procedure

* **Independent unit: the geographic block.** Training seeds share training data and are not independent replicates; inference seeds are repeated samples of the same tile.

| Set | Role | Tiles | Blocks | Smallest attainable p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GEN2 (Alcoi, Tarragona, Santa Barbara, Sacramento) | **primary** | 95 | 34 | Monte Carlo, 20 000 sign patterns |
| DEV‑B | secondary | 27 | 13 | 2^(1−13) = 0.00024 (exact) |
| GEN (Girona, Manresa, San Francisco) | secondary | 100 | 24 | Monte Carlo, 20 000 sign patterns |

  The protocol table lists DEV‑B as 27 / 14 and DEV‑A as 25 / 13, copied from the M13 documents. The stored block labels give 13 and 14. The analysis uses the stored labels; the protocol is not edited.
* **Per‑tile metrics** (`od_unreach_land`, dead‑end ratio, islands): average over inference seeds 1–3, take paired method differences, sum within blocks, average over training seeds TN_s0–s2. The estimate is Σ_b S_b / N_tiles.
  * **Test:** two‑sided block sign‑flip randomisation (Canay, Romano & Shaikh). Exact when B ≤ 16, else 20 000 random patterns. A reported p = 0.0000 means no sampled pattern was as extreme (p < 10⁻⁴).
  * **CI:** 95 % block‑bootstrap percentile interval, 2 000 resamples.
* **Set‑level metrics** (S10_cond, S_T, energy distance): paired block bootstrap for the CI and within‑block method‑label randomisation (2 000 permutations) for p. The smallest attainable p is then ≈ 0.002.
* **Non‑inferiority:** the upper 95 % CI limit of (method − reference) must be ≤ the margin.
* **Realism guard (H1, H3):** method S10 ≤ M\* S10 + 0.05 and density ratio within 1 ± 0.25.
* **Multiplicity:** Holm at α = 0.05 over the four GEN2 primary tests. DEV‑B and GEN are secondary and uncorrected.
* **Run sizes:** confirm runs have 3 training seeds × 3 inference seeds. H5, density‑matched, ablation, shift and H2 runs use inference seeds 1–2.

| S10_cond | 0.411 | 0.351 | 0.532 | 0.619 | 0.732 |

* **Rule.** The rise S10(4) − S10(0) must exceed the DEV‑B real‑vs‑real median (0.422), and the trend must be non‑decreasing in ≥ 3 of 4 steps.

## 5. Density: three analyses (protocol §7)

The single‑chain network samplers are denser than M\* (§3.1 of the failure analysis), so density is a candidate confounder. The conclusion requires sign agreement across three analyses.

| GEN2 contrast on `od_unreach_land` | Unmatched | Density‑adjusted intercept [95 % CI] | Density‑matched [95 % CI], p |
|---|---|---|---|
| NA‑fixed − M\* | −0.0259 | −0.0227 [−0.0325, −0.0137] | — (not in the matched plan) |
| NA‑SMC − M\* | −0.0247 | −0.0254 [−0.0384, −0.0116] | −0.0259 [−0.0438, −0.0073], p = 0.013 |
| NA‑myopic − M\* | −0.0269 | −0.0209 [−0.0323, −0.0115] | −0.0194 [−0.0325, −0.0073], p = 0.0006 |
| NA‑adaptive − M\* | −0.0242 | −0.0222 [−0.0350, −0.0111] | — |

* **Agreement.** The signs agree in all three analyses for every contrast, and every CI excludes 0. The adjustment slopes are small (−0.011 to −0.049 per unit log‑density difference).
* **Matching did not match on GEN2.** The DEV‑A stop biases gave GEN2 densities of 1.01 (A), 1.22 (M\*), 1.22 (NA‑myopic) and 1.30 (NA‑SMC). The matched contrasts are therefore matched only on DEV‑A (failure analysis §2.4).
* **S10 is not robust to matching.** In the matched runs, NA‑SMC − M\* S10 is +0.120 (p = 0.072) and NA‑myopic − M\* is −0.097 (p = 0.002), the reverse of the unmatched pattern. S10 conclusions between methods depend on density. Only the connectivity conclusion is claimed as density‑robust.

## 8. Threats to statistical validity

* **Few blocks on DEV‑B.** With 13 blocks the smallest p is 0.00024 and the bootstrap CIs are wide. The few‑cluster bootstrap is known to under‑cover (Cameron, Gelbach & Miller), which is why decisions use sign‑flip p together with the CI.
* **Training seeds are not independent.** They are averaged, never counted as replicates.
* **The OD metric samples pairs.** Its randomness is seeded per inference seed (`default_rng(1000 + seed)`). The same graph always gets the same value: duplicate runs are identical row for row.
* **Empty tiles count as OD = 1.** This is the pre‑registered convention. It dominates DEV‑A (2 of 25 tiles empty for every method) and adds ≈ 0.015 to every method on GEN (start‑head failure, failure analysis §3.4). The non‑empty variant is reported alongside.
* **Researcher degrees of freedom after confirmation.** Three analysis bugs were found and fixed after the confirmatory runs (failure analysis §4.4, §4.6, and the GEN contamination §4.1). Each fix makes the analysis follow the pre‑registered specification more closely, and no verdict changed.
