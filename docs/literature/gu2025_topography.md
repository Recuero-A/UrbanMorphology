## 3. What it measures (unverified)

A web-search summary (not an opened page) describes the study as follows:
- 100 cities worldwide;
- **ten topographic metrics** tested against road-network traits;
- terrain roughness and water area have negative effects on geometric fractal dimension and positive effects on circuity;
- mountain area correlates positively with orientation entropy.

**All of this is not verified.** The metric definitions, data and numbers are unknown.
