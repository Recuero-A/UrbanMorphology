## 4. What it measures (from the table of contents only)

The book offers a vocabulary and framework, not a dataset. It covers:
- street type and hierarchy (ch. 3);
- a pattern typology (ch. 4, "Pattern Type");
- route structure (ch. 5);
- connectivity and complexity measures (ch. 6).

The specific pattern-type labels and the connectivity indices defined in chapters 4–6 are **not verified**, because I had no legitimate copy. Louf & Barthelemy (2014, p. 1) cite the book as "a good account" of street-network classification. They criticise such classifications as containing "a large part of subjectivity".
